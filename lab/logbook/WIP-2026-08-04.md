## ⛔ FORBIDDEN fixes — the most expensive to re-derive

These came out of the adversarial audit. Each one is the remediation that the original finding
proposed, and each one breaks something:

1. **PB-8 — DO NOT add `HiveRole::Motherbee` to the `Worker` arm of `reconcile_egress`.** It would
   set the **control plane** to rewrite its own default route on every boot and every watchdog
   tick, from a yaml field, without rollback, on the box that provisions the egress. For a worker
   the failure is non-fatal by design; on motherbee it is a **lockout**. What goes in is a
   **read-only** check: never mutate the MB route, only compare it against `gateway_ip` and shout.
2. **U-4a — DO NOT reject peers by protocol version.** It is **self-fencing**: the cross-hive
   `SYSTEM_UPDATE` travels over the mesh and requires LSA visibility of the target, so cutting off
   the old peer cuts the only channel capable of fixing it. What goes in is **skew reporting**,
   nothing more.
3. **U-5 — DO NOT build `core_rollback` as "restore the most recent backup".** It would repeat U-1
   (reporting success without doing anything). Prerequisites: seal each generation with the
   `manifest_hash` it replaced, and make `rollback_local_core_binaries` **fail hard** when it does
   not find the backup. *(The part about creating empty directories on every no-op, and the
   retention, were already closed in `3246d82` as U-5b.)*
   **Watch out:** the fall-through of `rollback_local_core_binaries` **is unreachable today** —
   every caller passes `installed`, whose elements were already backed up. It only becomes
   reachable with the new caller. Frame it as *hardening a branch that this change makes
   reachable*, not as a prod bug, or someone will go off hunting a ghost.
4. **U-3 — DO NOT declare the manifest as a `conffile`.** On a generated JSON it leaves
   `.dpkg-dist`, asks, and merges nothing. Under non-interactive `apt`/`dpkg -i` the default is to
   **keep the old one**, whose `current` points to a directory that dpkg has just deleted → none
   of the base runtimes can start. *(Already solved in `d94f2ae` the right way.)*

## Contradictions to resolve when implementing

- **X1 — U-4a and U-5 do not agree on what "the core hash" is.** U-4a compares
  `core.manifest_hash` (what syncthing delivered); U-5 introduces `core.installed` precisely
  because that is **not** what is in `/usr/bin`. After U-5, a hive that was rolled back is
  **deliberately off-manifest**, and U-4a would print `verdict: "match"` for two hives running
  different binaries — a false "in sync" on the endpoint you check in the middle of an incident.
  Compare `core.installed.manifest_hash` when both sides report it.
- **X2** — U-4b's drift message must name **only** `update`, never `core_rollback`: the latter
  moves `/usr/bin` *away* from the manifest and would make the drift row permanent.
- **W4** — U-4b **must call** U-4a's `versions_comparison`, not reimplement it.

## How PB-7 turned out (implemented in `9f86c12`)

The survey's plan proposed chopping the three `add_hive` flows into ~8 blocking regions each
(~2500 lines). **It went another way:** the whole join runs in `spawn_blocking` with its own
`block_on`. That frees the admin channel —which is the bug— and turns the blocking `sleep`s from
executor poison into something legitimate, without restructuring 2500 lines.

Queryable state **reusing the vocabulary that already exists** in `info.yaml` (`pending` already
means "resumable") plus a `join:` block with a phase, instead of inventing a new admin action.

An invariant to preserve, already verified: `reconcile_hive_tls_material` and the public edge
selection in `sy_admin.rs` **filter by `status == "connected"`**, so an ingress in `joining` cannot
be chosen as the public door in the middle of a join.

And what must **not** be forgotten when moving the join to the background: the three B-2 revoke
queues have to travel with it. If they stay in `handle_admin`, a failed join leaves the
motherbee key and the NOPASSWD sudoers entry on the box **forever, silently**.
