# 2026-11-28 — the Digital Executive: solutions, capabilities and a WF at their door

The day before is in [2026-11-27](2026-11-27.md): stage 2 built and live, 0.1.157 to 0.1.161.
Times are ART, UTC−3, as [METHOD](METHOD.md) §1 has it.

## The talk (notes §33–§38)

- **An ED is a solution of Archi's CI.** System solutions ship with the software and Archi cannot
  edit them. SY.deploy only deploys solutions. Cloud reads the list and composes an ED from parts.
  This changes stage 2's call 1: the plain ED leaves SY.deploy's binary.
- **Cloud's screen:**
  - a list of EDs;
  - "create an ED", with its data and a timer;
  - capabilities added afterwards, each deployed on its own.
- **Three kinds:** `executive`, `capability`, `infrastructure`. Two attributes keep them three:
  origin (`system`, `catalog`, `custom`) and offered to.
- **A capability:**
  - it always works behind the ED, and may be several agents;
  - its IOs reach only external systems (the role `connector`);
  - a custom one is for the tenant, with an optional flag for all EDs or for named EDs.
- **The door, a WF** (the operator). An "ask capability" tool would clash with the pure-responder
  decision of 2026-10-27. It was found and brought to the operator before any design. The
  operator's answer is a WF at each capability's entry:
  - started only by the ED's conscious;
  - answering through a new `reply` action;
  - its help is its interface.
- **Consolidated:** [stage 3](../../docs/digital-executive-v1-stage-3.md), for the operator's
  review, with three points to call.

## Stage 3's design closed, and 3A and 3B started

- **The operator's last answers:**
  - a capability for all of a tenant's EDs, as proposed;
  - a request that takes long reaches the person (way a). The WF's timer wakes its instance, whose
    state holds the request's conversation (my judgment on the operator's timer idea); `notify`
    goes to the conscious, whose answer goes to the person's IO.
- **The operator: build.** Two builders in parallel, against one file of wire contracts:
  - 3A, the shared code: two more roles, `ask_capability` and the result turn;
  - 3B, the WF: `start_from` with consciouses, `reply`, `notify`, the guard, the help.

  The releases go in order, 3A before 3B.
