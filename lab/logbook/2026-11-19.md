## io.web stage 2.2's design talk (morning, to 13:00)

- **The Cloud page** for Fluxbee Cloud's team updated with stage 2.1's exposures and 0.1.149 (its
  version 11; the operator shares it).
- **The talk, with the operator** (chat only, the code read first): what is left of io.web was
  redefined, consolidated in [io-web-v2](../../docs/io-web-v2.md) §13.
  - io.web is for the website of a system (a backend, JavaScript, its own calls); artifacts are
    not io.web's.
  - Straight to the target, Archi inside Cloud's iframe, with no stage in its own tab: the system
    is in alpha, with no users.
  - A transparent proxy: Archi works as on its local port; Fluxbee adds the entry (the link and
    the cookie), the end (everything cut at once) and the permission to frame it; no time, size or
    rewrite of its own. My first proposal, limits per kind of upstream with figures, was the proxy
    changing the application's dynamics, and is withdrawn.
  - Everything under `fluxbee.ai` (Cloud on `cloud.fluxbee.ai` while it is developed), so the
    iframe is first-party and 2.1's cookie works in it as it is; the cookie cannot be avoided by a
    transparent proxy, and costs nothing more.
  - The operator's urgent points: SSE with no buffering (the edge relays frame by frame today, read
    in the code; io.web must too, and the gate measures the cadence); open connections cut at once
    when the access ends. The renewal: option (a), a session passes to the new enablement and a
    renewal interrupts nobody.
  - Third-party systems, to keep in mind (the operator's example, Odoo): WebSockets (on a second
    port with workers), `proxy_mode`, framing refused by its login page, SSO later.
  - A-142 decided for the proxy (no wait of its own); A-351 agreed.
- **The headers** (§13.7): Fluxbee's own answers keep today's strict set and add HSTS,
  `Permissions-Policy` and Cloud's origins as `frame-ancestors`; the application's answers keep
  their own headers, plus HSTS, `frame-ancestors` in a CSP header of its own, `nosniff` when
  missing, and no `Server` or `X-Powered-By`. **Cloud's origin** is a field of
  `publish_cloud_endpoint`, the operator's own proposal over a DNS TXT record (a new DNS library,
  and an answer not authenticated without DNSSEC); changing it rotates Cloud's token.
- **The `Origin` check** (§13.8, option (a)): io.web answers 403 to a write or a WebSocket that
  does not come from the exposure's own page (`Sec-Fetch-Site`, else `Origin`; Go 1.25's
  `CrossOriginProtection` algorithm), since every `*.fluxbee.ai` host is one site and
  `SameSite=Strict` stops no sibling; Archi's `/api/chat` takes a plain form today.
