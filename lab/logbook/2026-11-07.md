## What the gate found

- **A-161 🔴: a line a WF relays between two people names no one it was said to.** The agent reads
  `person A (<ILK>) via IO.gate0206a@worker1: "Otra vez vos? ..."`, exactly as a line said to
  itself; only its own lines say whom they were to (`you, to person A`). So:
  - at A's return, the agent took B's failure as its own: *"Ya me pasó de darle un dato sin
    respaldo, y no voy a repetirlo"*;
  - the episode, public news on every hive for 15 days, says A *"threatened to have the agent
    removed from the account"*;
  - asked to copy its lines, the agent left out B's two, *"que escribió por otro canal"*, though
    they were said to A.
  - The WF names the person as `dst_ilk` only toward the IO node of whoever started it (A-139).
    Toward B's it names no one: it does not know who is there.
- **The operator's choice:** name the destination. A relay keeps the IO node it went to, and the
  person when the message names one; the agent reads `person A via IO.a, to IO.b: "..."`, with a
  rule that a line said to someone else was not said to it; cognition's tagger and narrator read
  the same. For 0.1.105, and the gate again.
- **A-162 🔴:** one tagger call got an answer with no text, and the SDK's error does not say its
  `stop_reason`. That turn went unanalyzed. The error will name it, in 0.1.105.

## The build: A-161 and A-162, for 0.1.105

- **What it is** ([cognition-hands-v1](../../docs/cognition-hands-v1.md) §12.9):
  - a relay keeps where it went: `to_via`, the IO node, and `to`, the person its message names;
  - the agent reads `person A (<ILK>) via IO.gate0206a@worker1, to IO.gate0206b@worker1: "..."`,
    with a rule line ahead of the turns when any is a relay;
  - cognition's tagger reads whom a relay was written to, the narrator's rule says the counterpart
    is whom the turn was written to, and an episode of a relay is between its writer and the
    person its message names;
  - the SDK's error for an answer with no text names its `stop_reason`, or the Responses
    payload's `status`.
- The package's version stays 5: the new field is optional, and a package without it reads as
  before.
- **The review round** (one, one reviewer): two mediums and four lows, all fixed but one noted:
  - **Medium:** a message a WF forwards to an IO node and to the agent can come in two batches,
    since the router sends a batch as soon as it has a record. With the relay first, the agent
    would read a message it answered as not said to it. The agent's copy now makes the turn the
    agent's, and the worker's write-back keeps it so.
  - **Medium:** a relay whose message names no one gave the narrator no counterpart, or the
    thread's last other person, who could be the wrong one. It is now the last other person who
    wrote through the IO node it went to, else that node.
  - **Lows:** the narrator's rule covers the agent's own answer; an OpenAI answer cut for another
    reason says it; three tests more.
  - **Noted:** a WF's `forward` toward an IO node other than the person's keeps the `dst_ilk` its
    message had.
  - The 0.1.104 docs called two copies split across batches rare; they are not, and the docs now
    say what each case does.
