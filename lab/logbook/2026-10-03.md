## What came out of verifying before building

Two searches in the code, and the claims that mattered verified by hand:

- **A-44:**
  - The router did not clean up a connection that ended with an error: the entry stayed in the
    table and the socket stayed open, and a live node stayed connected with nobody reading its
    traffic. With "rechazar la nueva" (reject the new one) that would have been a permanent block,
    so it was fixed first.
  - The normal scheme does have duplicates, though they are short-lived: the double HELLO of every
    Go node at startup, and quick reconnections. They are absorbed with a 1 s wait.
  - The negative identity diag opened one connection per case.
- **A-45:** the `postinst` registers the runtimes in the dist manifest, and it counted on nobody
  writing it. That is why it is enough to stop the three that write it, and the orchestrator does
  the ordered restart with its boot order. No script is needed.
- **A-43:** no spawn path looked at the tenant. The check goes in the orchestrator, in the three
  places where it launches a node. In PROD, in 30 days IO.api did not serve a single request, and
  IO.wapp was still unconfigured.
- **A-16:** the 12 receive-only folders of the spokes were at 0 local changes.

  passed on: they went fine, with the ILK and the UUID file deleted.

- **Incident (B-16):**
  - The core updates went out before the motherbee router had the spokes back, and they failed
    with `TRANSPORT_ERROR`.

    one.

    retries, and judges by the installed hash.
