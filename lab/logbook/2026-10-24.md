# 2026-10-24 — 0.1.85: the edge proxies the web host to io.web

Where the track stands is in [io-web-v2](../../docs/io-web-v2.md); the facts of the release are in
the ledger's [0.1.85 entry](../DEPLOYMENTS.md). This page tells how it went.

## How it went

- **The operator's go-ahead** (2026-10-23, evening): "pásalo a prod completo para probar en
  entorno real". That covered the deploy and the one step by hand, `run_node IO.web@motherbee`.
- **Built in two steps, by agents in their own worktrees:**
  - **Step 1, the SDK's mesh module:** the mesh TLS material and the hive registry readers, one
    copy. It turned up a fifth reader of the registry, SY.vault, which moved too. It was
    committed and pushed alone (`5f796be`), and its CI was green before step 2 started.
  - **Step 2, three agents in parallel:** the edge's proxy, the io.web node, and `web_status`
    with the ops line.
    - The agents' worktrees started at `main` (`c3ac21ed`), not at the working branch, so each
      had to fast-forward to `5f796be` first. One noticed on its own; the other two were told.
    - Each left a few texts outside its files, which the integration fixed:
      - Archi's GET translation of `/web/status`;
      - five texts that still said only IO.cloud and IO.blob run in the root tenant, Archi's
        prompt among them.
- **The review round** (security, operations), run read-only on the integrated branch:
  - **Two medium findings, both fixed before the build:**
    - a resumed TLS session skipped io.web's role check;
    - a tenant's io.web could take port 9200.
  - **Three low ones fixed;** two deferred to stage 2 (A-89, A-90).
  - **One divergence recorded for the IO family's review (A-91):** io.web boots CONFIGURED,
    while its siblings boot UNCONFIGURED and work.
- **A small lesson in the agents' tooling:** three wait loops never ended.
  - They waited with `pgrep -f "cargo test …"`, and each one's own command line contained that
    text, so each found itself.
  - The loops sat idle until killed; nothing else depended on them.
- **The deploy and the gate** found nothing to fix:
  - **Before io.web ran:** the Archi host answered the fixed 502 with its request id.
  - **After `run_node`:**
    - the page came through from outside;
    - worker1 and egress1 were refused in the handshake, and ingress1 was admitted;
    - a query never reached a log;
    - the edge and io.web logged the same request id;
    - the restarts and the core-IO refusals behaved as designed;
    - A-81's publication from worker1 still worked.
