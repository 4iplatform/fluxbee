## The chat: where cognition keeps its state

Reviewed whole with the operator, in three rounds (cognition-hands-v1 §11).

- **Can a node move to another hive?** Not today:
  - the hive is part of a node's name;
  - since 0.1.83 SY.identity refuses an orchestrator that registers another hive's node ILK;
  - and a node's own state lives in its folder on its hive: an agent's thread state, a WF's
    instances, io.slack's thread records.

  The operator: a node is born and dies on its hive, *"nunca pensé en lo contrario"*. What would
  move one day is a digital agent's soul, not a node.
- **What cognition does today,** read in the code:
  - it keeps nothing on its own disk but its config;
  - its state goes to 8 tables in SY.storage, up to ~100 rows a turn;
  - a worker's cognition skips a turn when the motherbee does not answer;
  - after a host reboot, each conversation's first message reaches its agent with no memory.

  Recorded as A-158.
- **Decided:**
  - cognition keeps its state in its own SQLite file, which only it may read;
  - the message carries what serves the communication, so no per-turn result goes to SY.storage;
  - what cognition keeps is online state, so a closed item is deleted;
  - a communication is never deleted: *"alguien muere y nadie lo olvida"*;
  - a lost hive is covered by the snapshots;
  - the cognition tables on 8.x go.
- **A chapter of its own:** an ILK is disabled, and deleted only when nothing refers to it
  (A-159), for later.
- **A correction:** §10.6's proposal said an agent could come up on another hive as the same ILK.
  That has been wrong since 0.1.83.

## The build: the rest of A-137

The operator's go-ahead, with four calls (consolidated in cognition-hands-v1 §12.3, 9 to 12):

- a solution runs on one hive, a rule for Archi and not a preference: SY.wf-rules refuses a
  definition naming another hive's agent or WF, and the handbook says so (§4.2.7);
- the news belong to their protagonist and are not copied: the agent is told, answers
  accordingly, and never repeats them, so *"no terminemos en un chismerío total"*;
- cognition's broadcast, after a look at the policy: the router's broadcast already reaches every
  hive's SY.cognition, and the system policy gates only its protected actions;
- a news item is a paragraph, not a word: no topics, the agent sorts it out.

What it is (§12.7):

- **The router** copies at entry what a local WF sends an IO node, the WF as producer.
- **SY.cognition:**
  - a relay joins the communication of the agent that served the thread last on its hive, or the
    thread's with no agent, which the region leaves out and whose writer pays;
  - one message copied twice is one turn (`turn_key`);
  - the narrator's call may propose an episode, gated as in the POC. A kept episode goes to the
    file, to the book in memory and, by the router's broadcast, to every hive's cognition. It
    fades with time (a 7-day half-life) and leaves under 2;
  - the package's version 5: the feelings and up to 5 news of the people in the conversation;
  - the file's state version 2: the rows of 0.1.103 start again.
- **ai.generic** shows the news with their rule: take them into account, never repeat them.
- **SY.storage** takes the `writer` source, for SY.cognition only.
- **Small fixes that rode along:** the stale note in cognition's `STATUS`, and the empty folders
  of before its file.
- **The review round** (one, two reviewers, cognition and the rest):
  - **No high finding.**
  - **A medium:** a WF that sends a person's first message to the agent and to another person at
    once had the relay join the communication with no agent. It was analyzed and paid twice. A
    batch's relays now find their communication after its other turns are held.
  - **Five lows, fixed:**
    - who served a thread last is the newest turn;
    - the agent's answer relayed in a later cycle is no second turn;
    - the news reach all of a conversation's people;
    - the communications with no agent are neither loaded at start nor counted as pruned;
    - the package's `dropped_feelings` takes a default, for the upgrade's window.
  - **Noted, not changed:** a definition applied before is checked at its next apply. No WF was
    deployed on 8.x.
- **Checks:**
  - both Rust workspaces: fmt, clippy denying warnings, tests;
  - the Go modules touched: gofmt, vet, tests;
  - the CI guards that run on a Mac;
  - the docs' links.
