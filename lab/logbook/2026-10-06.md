## Stage 2: Syncthing addresses and folders

**Built and reviewed four times** (adversarial reviews of the diff; none found anything that would
break the upgrade from 0.1.57):

1. **First:** the `config.xml` writer followed symlinks planted in the Syncthing user's directory
   (major); also, a lock held during REST calls with no timeout, ownership by name instead of by
   registered ID, and backfills in the middle of a join.
2. **Second:** the A-50 folder rule came from the `hive.yaml` flags; turning a sync off for a while
   left all the spokes without folders forever (major). Plus: structural parsing (devices with no
   name), a reader guarded against FIFOs and hard links, a pending restart persisted.
3. **Third:** no majors. A new folder cloned the members of another one, or was seeded with a peer;
   `--device-id` does not exist in Syncthing v2 (A-53); the join queue wrote without a lock.
4. **Fourth:** limited to the last delta; minors only. The fix for the join queue could leave a
   permanent `failed` if the join was retried within a gap of seconds: it was reverted, and the
   backfill now waits for an explicit `join.phase: done`. `device-id` is now bounded to 5 s.

**A-50, found in PROD with `lab/posture-check.py`:** the motherbee shared `blob/active` with the
egress (pending on the egress side; no data traveled). Fixed in stage 2.

**Baseline 0.1.57** (`lab/posture-check.py`, read-only, on the 4 VMs): ingress1 with a fixed
address on the motherbee, worker1 with the motherbee `dynamic`, egress1 with no device ID in the
registry and inside `fluxbee-blob`. This is exactly what stage 2 has to change.

## 0.1.62 → live: the identity replica no longer hangs (P7-1, A-59), A-57, D32 follow-up

- **The problem, confirmed live before the fix:** the identity subscription of worker1 had gone
  15,929 s without a packet in either direction.
- **The fix:** a heartbeat from the primary every 20 s without traffic. The replica cuts the
  connection after 60 s of silence and subscribes again.
- **Validation with a real 100 s cut:** the replica reported it at 60 s and reconnected 5 s after
  the cut was lifted.
- **The adversarial review found an old race:** a delta could get lost between the snapshot and the
  stream when a subscription opened. It was fixed at the root: register before answering and before
  each snapshot.
- **A-57:**
  - Syncthing restarts when its binary or its unit changes, or when its process runs a deleted
    binary.
  - Tested with an identical copy of the binary under the running process: one restart, and the
    next update with no restart.
- **Join from zero (VM 104 re-cloned):**
  - it waited 8.5 min for cloud-init and ended `connected`;
  - SSH only inside the join;
  - leaf issued before the first SSH;
  - vendor fatal.
  - Afterwards: hive removed and VM powered off.
- **Interruption:** the usage-limit cutoff hit in the middle of the guard probes. They had already
  finished and the file was restored, so nothing had to be repeated.
- **The operator's decision:** D33 = (a). The egress door opens with a key stored in the vault, and
  only the egress accepts it; the rest are reached through the console. It goes into revision 11.
