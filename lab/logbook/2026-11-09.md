## The shape of 3c, decided from the code

- **The write travels as `SYSTEM_UPDATE` category `config`,** with a `write`, to the motherbee's
  orchestrator. That orchestrator is the only writer.
  - Policy rule (2) already admits SY.admin@motherbee toward every orchestrator.
  - A new message type would have needed the router's policy, which ships alone.
- **Every hive applies a version** with the usual form (version and hash). It answers
  `sync_pending` until its copy matches. Then it restarts only the start readers whose engine
  changed: SY.cognition, and SY.frontdesk.gov on the motherbee.
- **SY.admin's executor and Archi are never restarted by a config update.** They re-read the file
  at their next use, as ai.generic does at each request.
  - Archi may be the caller, and a restart would cut its reply and the operator's chat.
  - This replaces the design's "SY.admin restarts last".
- **Who reaches what:**
  - `set_installation_ai` is the operator's word: loopback, or a plan Archi stages and the
    operator confirms.
  - `get_installation` is open to Archi.

## The build and its review round

- **The build:** one agent on `coa/hive-config-s3c`, CI green; about 3,800 lines of code.
  - SY.admin's diff first read as +6,338/−4,274. That was the default diff algorithm's pairing;
    `--diff-algorithm=histogram` shows +2,074/−10.
- **The review round:** two reviewers in parallel, one on what the change could break (the
  deploy runs through the same paths) and one on the new paths. No high.
- **Three mediums:**
  - A vault that did not answer during the next-use refresh left Archi or the executor without
    AI, with no retry.
  - A stale apply record could leave a reader on the wrong engine after a failure and an undo.
  - A switch of the default provider never checked the tenants' keys.
- **Lows:**
  - hives whose join did not finish were skipped without being named;
  - some hints pointed to fixes that cannot work;
  - a wrong hash read as `sync_pending` forever;
  - a write that times out can still land.
- **Kept:** the apply restarts readers inline, a few seconds. `NOT_PRIMARY` answers 409.
- **An older bug, found by the review and confirmed in SY.admin's journal on 8.x (A-197):**
  `list_admin_actions` over the mesh did not fit the frame (337 KB against 128 KiB).
  - It had happened 30 times in 7 days.
  - Archi's plan compiler had been running with an empty catalog.
  - It went into this release, on its own branch stacked on 3c's: it touches the same binaries,
    and it is the operator's way to the new action through Archi.
  - Its fix listed the other SY.admin mesh replies that can outgrow the frame: A-198, observed.
- **The fixes:** the same agent, on 3c's branch (F1–F7) and on `coa/a-197`, CI green on both.
  The agent's decisions I confirmed:
  - skipped hives do not make the answer `partial`, as `/versions` leaves them out;
  - systemd's monotonic activation stamp decides whether a reader is known;
  - the generic guard for oversized replies waits (A-198).
