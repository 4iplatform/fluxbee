## Found in the gate

- **An empty `wf: {}` block:** a WF message whose action has no `vars`, `menu` or `subject`

## Decided: at most 10 timers in a WF definition (A-153)

- Talking over A-151's gate, the operator: a WF with more than 10 or 20 timers is not sane.
- A node's count is its open conversations times their timers, since an instance cancels its
  timers when it ends. So the limit goes on the definition, per conversation: at most 10 timers,
  checked when the WF is written.

## Decided: a quiet WF conversation closes on its own (A-154)

- Nothing closes a running instance whose flow never reaches an end: it stays open, and a message
  weeks later takes up the old flow.
- The operator: the system closes it, after 4 days by default, which a definition may change up to
  7 days. The WF does it for each conversation, in its hourly pass.
- Quiet means no activity and no pending timer: a conversation that waits on its own timer, like the
  handbook's 30-day due date, is not abandoned.
