# 2026-10-21 — Let's Encrypt for the edge (0.1.82)

PROD's public door now serves its own Let's Encrypt certificate, issued and renewed by SY.admin.
Ledger: [0.1.82](../DEPLOYMENTS.md). Design: [acme-edge-certificates-v1](../../docs/acme-edge-certificates-v1.md).

## How it went

- **The operator first wanted to understand certificates.** An explainer page, private to the
  operator, covered certificates, the chain, ACME, the three challenges, TLS-ALPN-01, lifetimes and
  rate limits.
- **Decisions on 2026-10-20:**
  - D1–D5: the client on the motherbee, hot reload now, renewal by ARI with relative alerts, the
    `classic` profile, staging first.
  - **Open source:** the domain never comes from code. The name is the host of
    `admin.public_base_url`, the CA comes from `acme:`, and the egress probe target from
    `hive.yaml`, with no default.
- **Verifying the code before the design turned up A-78:** SY.vault's change notice never reached
  the edge, so PB-1's "restart on change" had never run. PROD's journals confirmed it.
- **The design** got one review round from three reviewers.
  - The review changed it in substance:
    - ACME replaces only what it issued;
    - nothing is written while the edge does not serve the last write;
    - a trust-path check before storing;
    - one order at a time;
    - the edge sees only its own notices.
  - The operator then approved the PROD steps and accepted Let's Encrypt's terms.
- **The code:**
  - four agents by area, then one review round of three reviewers;
  - the high finding was that SY.vault's rollback swaps versions, so a retried rollback could undo
    itself. It now rolls back only its own write, identified by version and `updated_at`.
- **PROD:** in this order, each step approved:
  1. the chain check of the wildcard;
  2. the deploy;
  3. A-78 seen live;
  4. `acme:` and an SY.admin restart;
  5. the staging test (8 s);
  6. the production certificate (7 s);
  7. strict checks inside and out;
  8. a rollback both ways, which the edge followed on the vault's notice alone.
