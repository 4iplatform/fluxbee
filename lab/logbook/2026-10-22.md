# 2026-10-22 — Control-path hardening (0.1.83)

Root on a spoke can no longer command the whole installation through the doors this batch closed.
Ledger: [0.1.83](../DEPLOYMENTS.md). Design:
[control-path-hardening-v1](../../docs/control-path-hardening-v1.md). Findings: A-62, A-20, A-22,
A-42, A-46, A-55, A-79 (new, resolved), A-80 (new, open).

## How it went

- **The operator's decisions:**
  - the batch, by cost/benefit: A-62, A-20, A-22 (the cheap part), the minimal stage 4 and A-42,
    plus the simple technical debt;
  - every host-level rule is applied by the product: by the orchestrator at add_hive and on its
    reconcile, never by hand on a hive;
  - A-42 rule (b): only an email the channel vouches for merges;
  - SSH on the ingress only from inside, the egress as today with a switch;
  - the debt in full, with a clippy gate in CI.
- **The design review** (security, operations) found three more doors for the same attack: the
  orchestrator's admin inbox, SY.identity's orchestrator scope, and the router installing
  whatever a spoke's LSA advertised. Closing A-62 alone would not have reached the goal.
- **The operator then decided:**
  - the three doors go in this release;
  - SSH to the ingress only from the motherbee, matched by source address rather than by
    interface (one NIC cannot tell inside from outside);
  - other networks only when add_hive lists them.
- **The code:**
  - five agents, each in its own worktree and its own files;
  - one review round of three reviewers (security, operations, correctness). Its two serious
    findings were older router behavior that the batch's guarantees depend on: a WAN frame's
    sender was not bound to its session, and a hive's LSA could shadow another hive's node
    UUIDs. Both were fixed with the rest (A-79);
  - 1,573 tests pass, with no build warnings.
- **PROD:**
  - deploy in 251 s;
  - the gate: the refusals from a throwaway mesh client, a node's life ending in `teardown_node`,
    Archi, Cloud's `register_human`, a workflow deploy, ACME staging, the ingress's SSH from each
    hive.

## Still open

- **A-80:** a restore is refused when another tenant holds the same email.
- **The next release:** drop `turns.ctx`/`seq` and their index, then the `ALTER`.
- **Not offered yet:** the operator merging two complete people.
- **Recorded as residuals (design §9):**
  - the ingress terminates TLS, so its root holds the Cloud surface;
  - `src_ilk` is not bound to the sender;
  - the two-way `blob/active` folder;
  - the unsigned apt repository (A-49);
  - the loopback HTTP APIs on the motherbee;
  - a workflow's next version from another hive while its nodes are stopped.
- **The last debt step:** `clippy --fix`, the remaining Spanish comments and the CI gate go in
  their own commit, after this release.
