## The talk, the design and its panel

- **The talk** (2026-11-20 to today), recorded as it went in
  [digital-executive-v1](../../docs/digital-executive-v1.md), with the operator's words. The operator's
  way: the details recorded as they are talked, then all reviewed, and only then a solution.
- **The design:** [digital-executive-v1-design](../../docs/digital-executive-v1-design.md). The ED is
  a virtual person: a person's ILK with a `virtual` flag. It is born from Fluxbee Cloud through
  SY.admin and a new CD node, SY.deploy. Its responsibilities are deployments of Archi's solutions,
  in three layers with blanks. Its channels are IO nodes assigned to it by configuration. Its
  conversations are joined by the pair. Its people's profile is in a blob named in the SHM. Its
  identity on the internet comes in stages.
- **The panel** (five lenses: the code's facts, fidelity, identity and security, CD and operation,
  messaging and cognition) found:
  - one blocker, the profile blob's name;
  - two high holes:
    - an unbound `src_ilk` that EDs would act on;
    - a stranger merging into an ED through its LLM-read mailbox;
  - many gaps.

  All were applied to the design. The operator decided on each the same day (design §17.6): yes to
  all, with ED–ED talk out of the first stage for now.
- **The order of the work** (design §15): the base of identity and security first. Its build design:
  [digital-executive-v1-stage-1](../../docs/digital-executive-v1-stage-1.md). The operator's calls:
  - IO, WF and every SY may send a person's ILK;
  - report first, then enforce;
  - 8.x may be broken or reinstalled.
