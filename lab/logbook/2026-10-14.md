# 2026-10-14 — The ILK is the actor and the tenant a cost center: stages 1 and 2 in PROD (0.1.65 and 0.1.66)

## The operator's decisions

- **The model (operator, Claude and Astra):**
  - The ILK identifies the actor and is never duplicated because of a tenant: *"la identidad dentro
    del sistema es prioridad a todo, no veo bien resolver esto creando miles de ilk para lo mismo"*
    (identity inside the system is the priority over everything; I don't think it is right to solve
    this by creating thousands of ilks for the same thing).
  - The tenant is a cost center, a spending record: *"el tenant es un registro de gastos, quién paga
    es problema del cloud"* (the tenant is a record of expenses; who pays is the cloud's problem).
    Companies, members, billing and the legal side belong to Cloud.
  - Permissions are explicit: changing the tenant of an ILK neither grants nor removes anything.
  - A suspended tenant does not use the infrastructure.

  How it came about: *"la idea la hicimos entre los tres, sin la charla no sale NADA más que código
  basura"* (the three of us made the idea together; without the talk NOTHING comes out but junk
  code). Astra evaluated the code against the idea.
- **Cloud deletes and restores only the ILKs of people** (D4: "sí" (yes)).
- **The order:** ILK structure, firm tenant, Cloud, and tokens at the end. *"Arrancá donde te
  parezca."* (Start wherever you see fit.)
- **The document:** `docs/ilk-tenant-model-v1.md` is the only source; FINDINGS (A-63) and the
  ledger point there.
- **For the Cloud team:** the ticket-by-ticket answer and the new model, published as a private page
  for Noe. The operator shares it.
- **The operator's Anthropic key** is in the vault as `anthropic_root_pool`, to test Archi with both
  models later on.

## Stage 1: the ILK structure (A-63)

- **Design review (one round):** 7 majors and 5 minors, folded in before writing code.
- **The code:**
  - the scope (`scope`) of each channel fixes its key, so reassigning an ILK moves none of them;
  - one Cloud account per email, in the install's scope, which only IO.cloud writes;
  - `ILK_SET_TENANT`, with history in the same transaction;
  - the v3 migration, which runs when the primary boots, before it loads memory.
- **Code review (one round):** 1 major and 8 minors, all fixed.
  - **The major:** purging a tenant that a person had left always failed, because of the FK from
    that person's channels to the tenant. Now the purge takes those channels with it.
  - **The minors:** details in FINDINGS A-63.
- **Tests:** identity 94, admin 115, SDK 180, io-cloud 18, io-common 109.

## Stage 2: the secrets (A-64, 0.1.66)

- **The talk with the operator:** three rounds, and in two of them the operator corrected me.
  - *"tenant no es identidad ni seguridad"* (tenant is neither identity nor security): the tenant of
    a secret is who pays, and the vault only checks that it is active.
  - The general token stays: *"es un recurso fácil de usar"* (it is an easy resource to use), and
    setting it is Cloud's decision.
  - A person can own secrets, for example the access to an ERP.
  - A node is born and dies in its tenant.
  - At genesis only the core is born, and the orchestrator defines it.
  - No groups, and no general key for the whole install.

  Unfinished registrations are read as such (D11, in `80b479c`).
- **What I found in the code while we talked:**
  - the identity SHM already carried the real tenant status; my doc said the opposite;
  - a company's AI node could never use the root key that A-35 promised.
- **Review (one round):** 1 major and 9 minors.
  - **The major:** refreshing the value of a token without naming nodes turned it general.
  - **The minors:** fixed or documented; details in FINDINGS A-64.
- **Build:** the first one died of memory on the build VM (8 GB, no swap). An 8 GB swapfile was
  added, and the second one passed in 18 min.
- **Gate:** measured with the vault audit, which records every read by every node. Assigned,
  removed, general, suspended and reactivated, and the secret of a person who changes company: all
  as designed. Details in `lab/DEPLOYMENTS.md`.
