## How it went

- **The build:** two agents built §5.2.1. One took the WF engine and its language, the other
  ai.generic and its SDK.
- **The review round:** five reviewers, each blind to the others' lens: the engine, security, the
  language, the docs and the agents. What mattered most:
  - **One person's message could bounce between sales and invoice without end** (security). A
    handback asked sales again with the same message, and a probe made 101 agent requests from one
    message. Now a handback delivers invoice's answer and waits for the person: one message costs
    two agent calls at most. The engine has no cap of its own yet (A-125, now with Case 3).
  - **An agent in `start_from` would have its late answer start an instance**, A-118's loop again
    (docs, engine, security). The spec's case study had exactly that shape. `start_from` refuses
    `AI.*`.
  - **A blank text under a `text_field` broke a person's later turns** (agents): it left a blank
    turn in the agent's history, which Anthropic refuses. The history keeps the whole object now.
  - **A pending payment check could die before its date.** An agent's error or timeout failed the
    instance, timers included. Errors and timeouts now go back to sales, and the timer stays.
- **After the round, by me** (the agent side):
  - a fenced object followed by text is that object;
  - the fallback instruction asks for no null;
  - a failed parse logs the model's first 200 chars;
  - two test names collided between concurrent cargo runs (sy_orchestrator's backup dirs and its
    askpass file), and now they cannot.
- **CI, locally, before the commit:**
  - Rust: fmt, clippy and the whole workspace's tests;
  - Go: gofmt, vet and the race tests of the six modules;
  - the doc links.

  All clean.
- **The release:** build of `75a6786` in 19 min, deploy in 236 s, health clean. `wf.engine` and
  `ai.generic` are at 0.1.91 on the motherbee and worker1, and Archi upgraded its seeds.

- **Record what the system's use turns up, the test's or not:** *"si encontrás al usar el sistema
  problemas no documentados, seguí con la prueba pero documentá los bugs como pendientes, el uso
  del sistema que hacés ayuda a debug"*. The gate had worked around three problems that were not in
  FINDINGS. Each was checked in the code and is now an open entry:
  - **A-126:** Archi deletes an agent asset that an agent's definition still names. The agent keeps
    working until its next start, and then runs without that asset. That is why the cleanup purged
    the agents before their roles.
  - **A-127:** SY.admin's `control/config-get`, `control/config-set` and `messages` routes send a

    `status`, `help`, `restart` and `DELETE` on the same path take the bare name.
  - **A-128:** a wf-rules `check` is a compile, and stages the definition. A later `apply` without
    a version would deploy it. It goes with WF Case 5's check before deploy.
- **Leave a track's gate deployment up:** *"no borres el despliegue de los nodos… hasta tener todo
  el caso completo y después, solo por las dudas, lo hacés borrando todo y corriendo de cero"*.
  The Case 1 set was already removed. It is deployed again for Case 3 and stays up until the track

## Case 3, decided with the operator

A design conversation, in chat, one problem at a time. Everything is now in
[wf-orchestration-v1](../../docs/wf-orchestration-v1.md) §5.3.

- **Escalation is two cases:**
  - the internal consultation, Case 3: the supervisor decides, and the agent stays the voice;
  - handing the conversation to a person of the team, Case 4.

  Archi writing and operating WFs becomes Case 5.
- **The menu.** What an agent may do is fixed and clear, and never in its role: *"me molesta que
  esté en el prompt"*. The WF sends the options in the message, each WF its own menu.
  - The menu has a bounded `help` and the options with their params.
  - `none` is always an answer.
  - An answer off the menu is an error, with no retries: *"no hacer cosas raras"*.
- **The catch, as in .NET:** *"podés programar qué querés que haga, o el default, que ahora sería
  log"*. The cap on agent calls (A-125) comes with it.
- **The way back belongs to the IO node,** by the operator's principle: *"el que responde tiene
  que ser dueño o saber más que nadie del dato para decidir"*. io.slack records which thread each
  of its Slack threads belongs to.
- **Archi learns this from its handbook,** and the handbook becomes the one reference: *"Fluxbee
  necesita un handbook único, sería el de Archi"*. The handbook for all of Fluxbee is proposed as
  its own topic, after this track.
- **The operator:** *"no tomar otro tema hasta terminar este"*.
