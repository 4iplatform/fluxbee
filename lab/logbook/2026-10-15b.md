# 2026-10-15 (night) — Stage 4: Cloud manages its tenants and its nodes (0.1.68 and 0.1.69)

## The operator's decisions

- **A tenant is not a company:** it is a cost center, a workspace or deployment of the system, an
  internal ID. Cloud maps each company to a tenant (one to one today); Fluxbee sees only the tenant.
- **D13:** only an email joins two ILKs; with the same email there is a merge, triggered by what the
  person provides; meanwhile two ILKs coexist. Within a tenant the merge already exists; across
  tenants it stays open, not for now.
- **Stage 4 (D14):** Cloud is the business layer.
  - It does everything with the tenants except the genesis one, and with the people (registered by
    the frontdesk) it also deletes and restores.
  - It manages the credentials, everything except seeing them.
  - It launches IO nodes only to have somewhere to load the token, and it can start, stop and
    delete the ones it launched.
  - The infrastructure belongs to the operator, through Archi.
- **Rules that stand:**
  - every user action goes through the admin; a node does not act on another one, with some
    exceptions;
  - the IO nodes come from different eras (io.linkedhelper and io.wapp are test-grade): a general
    review of the IO family when this topic is finished.
