# 2026-10-23 — io.web v2: the direction (design only)

Where the track stands is in [io-web-v2](../../docs/io-web-v2.md); this page only tells how it got
there. Nothing was built.

## How it went

- **2026-10-22, evening.**
  - Three read-only surveys of the code mapped:
    - the blob and IO.blob;
    - how the edge publishes;
    - io.web's history.
  - A first draft, *edge-publishing-v1*, extended the artifact circuit to sites, uploads through
    the edge and an inbox from the ingress to the core. Two reviews hardened it.
  - The operator stopped it: files move only through Syncthing, nodes carry references, the edge
    publishes URLs.
  - It was never committed. What it proposed is listed as withdrawn in io-web-v2 §8, and its
    reviews' lessons in §7.
- **2026-10-23, a conversation without agents.**
  - **What is needed:** Archi as a URL for Fluxbee Cloud, and artifacts. There is no upload path:
    files come through their channels, and Archi's page does its own uploads.
  - **What was missing:** the edge's third surface, the real reverse proxy of the io.web beta,
    was never built. Today the edge relays channel messages (64 KiB, JSON only) and serves
    single-file artifacts.
  - **Agreed:**
    - one door (the edge, a reverse proxy) and several managers (IO nodes, each with its own
      security);
    - io.web behind the proxy, on the motherbee next to Archi, reached over an internal HTTP
      channel with mutual TLS (the mesh socket cannot carry web traffic);
    - artifacts served by the edge from its Syncthing copy;
    - no manager on the ingress, and no new hive.
  - **How to continue (the operator):** treat io.web as a first-class citizen of every leg of
    Fluxbee, not as a security problem only, and build from the outside in, the skeleton first.
