# 2026-10-15 — The firm tenant in PROD (0.1.67, stage 3) and the vault binds the ILK to the sending node (A-65)

## The operator's decisions (2026-10-14, at night)

- **No person is deleted or disappears because of what happens to their company:** *"no quiero
  borrar ni desaparecer ilk de humanos"* (I don't want to delete or make disappear the ilks of
  humans). Deleting a company disables it; restoring it means reactivating it.
- **On nodes and resources, action is needed:** *"sobre nodos y recursos si vale la pena actuar"*
  (on nodes and resources it is worth acting). The orchestrator does not start or restart the nodes
  of a company that is not active; it does not kill the ones running; no cut in the router.
- **io.api `by_ilk`:** option (b), the people the company knows (D12).
- **The contacts:** whoever writes to a company is charged to it (*"si hay alguien que se comunica
  es asociado a un evento sobre ese TNT"* (if someone communicates, it is associated with an event on
  that TNT)). Joining a contact to an account, for example a customer of A who becomes the owner of
  B, stays open (D13).
- **A TNT is almost always a company, but not always.**
- **A-65:** fix it now (*"Sí, de acuerdo"* (Yes, agreed)).
