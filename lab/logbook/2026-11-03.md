## Case 5, decided

A design conversation in chat, one topic at a time. Everything is in §5.5.

- **Archi's model:**
  - Archi runs on Claude Opus 5.5. The operator: *"así programa la solución ese modelo"*. Until the
    build, the whole motherbee runs on Opus, so the operator can watch its costs. Then Opus is for
    Archi alone, through a model of its own in its config.
  - Archi has a key of its own, `anthropic_architect`. The operator made it at Anthropic and handed
    it over by a file's path. A node takes the key it owns before its tenant's general one, so no
    code changed (ledger 0.1.94).
  - Its limits go up in the build: 32,000 tokens per answer, 1,000,000 per pipeline task, and 600 s
    per call.
- **Topic 1, the handbook's atoms:**
  - one small case per mechanism, each a definition that compiles;
  - the operator: a whole case is never a normal pattern. From Case 4, what is extracted is
    redirecting to another agent, escalating, and the like.
- **Topic 2, the check before deploy:** `wf_rules_check`, as OPA's `opa_check` is. Archi checks
  every definition before showing it, and a refusal goes back to whoever wrote the definition.
  - While building: whoever wrote it is the designer, since the definition is in the manifest. The
    programmer builds only runtime packages.
- **Topic 3, deploying without breaking what runs:**
  - a first deploy always creates the node;
  - an update restarts the node once;
  - a removal says how many conversations are running, and goes ahead on the operator's
    confirmation.
- **Topic 4, operating instances:** mine to decide (*"primero miralo vos"*). SY.admin gets
  `wf_list_instances`, `wf_get_instance` and `wf_cancel_instance`.
- **Solution development is a chapter of its own** (the operator): a document on developing
  solutions, a handbook of good development practices, and for each solution a place on disk, a
  minimal manifest and how to deploy it. *"No quisiera que nazca a medias"*. Meanwhile each case
  starts from zero.
- **The gate is mine to run:** *"no me esperes a mí para esta prueba, confío en que si el bug no
  sale ahora, explota mañana"*.

## The gate (§5.5.3)

- **From zero:** the old gate was removed (WF gate0192, three agents, their roles), and a team
  member was registered through Cloud. The person and the team run as gate clients on worker1
  (`IO.gate0195@worker1`, `IO.gate0195team@worker1`), each with its ILK and its ICH.
- **Archi, in the operator's words** (sales, invoicing and its return, a consulted discount with
  3 minutes, a credit note to the team with 5 minutes of quiet, a due invoice for collections):
  - it created three roles, a skill, and the workflow `ventas-gate0195` from the atoms;
  - it checked the workflow with SY.wf-rules (`wf_rules_check`, 7 states and 39 guards) before
    every plan;
  - it stopped to ask when it could not read the two IO nodes' config to point them at the WF,
    since they are clients with no config. The operator said to leave them.
- **What the gate surfaced, fixed at the source:**
  - A-143: an identity write through SY.admin works only on the motherbee. Archi set the roles
    with `hive: worker1`, and the replica refused. Fixed in 0.1.96: writes go to the primary.
  - A-144: the workflow's deploy outgrew SY.admin's executor model, which had to repeat the whole
    definition, and Archi's chat said "finished". Fixed in 0.1.96: a step with nothing to fill
    goes as planned with no model, and the chat verifies the run.
    - On 0.1.96 the chat answered `error`, as it should, but the direct dispatch did not route
      `wf_rules_compile_apply` (`UNKNOWN_ACTION`, a latent bug of the executor's fallback). Fixed
      in 0.1.97 with the tool's own dispatch, and a test that sends the step to a fake
      SY.wf-rules.
- **A-145, found next:** on 0.1.97 the workflow's name `ventas-gate0195` passed the check and
  failed the package's publish, since the runtime policy has no hyphen. Archi removed the broken
  workflow. 0.1.98 gives every name rule the runtime policy. Archi then deployed it as
  `ventas.gate0195`, and the node ran.
- **The first run, on 0.1.98:** all of it passed (§5.5.3).
