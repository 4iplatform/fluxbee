## Case 5, decided

A design conversation in chat, one topic at a time. Everything is in §5.5.

- **Archi's model:**
  - Archi runs on Claude Opus 5.5. The operator: *"así programa la solución ese modelo"*. Until the
    build, the whole motherbee runs on Opus, so the operator can watch its costs. Then Opus is for
    Archi alone, through a model of its own in its config.
  - Archi has a key of its own, `anthropic_architect`. The operator made it at Anthropic and handed
    it over by a file's path. A node takes the key it owns before its tenant's general one, so no
    code changed (ledger 0.1.94).
  - Its limits go up in the build: 32,000 tokens per answer, 1,000,000 per pipeline task, and 600 s
    per call.
- **Topic 1, the handbook's atoms:**
  - one small case per mechanism, each a definition that compiles;
  - the operator: a whole case is never a normal pattern. From Case 4, what is extracted is
    redirecting to another agent, escalating, and the like.
- **Topic 2, the check before deploy:** `wf_rules_check`, as OPA's `opa_check` is. Archi checks
  every definition before showing it, and a refusal goes back to whoever wrote the definition.
  - While building: whoever wrote it is the designer, since the definition is in the manifest. The
    programmer builds only runtime packages.
- **Topic 3, deploying without breaking what runs:**
  - a first deploy always creates the node;
  - an update restarts the node once;
  - a removal says how many conversations are running, and goes ahead on the operator's
    confirmation.
- **Topic 4, operating instances:** mine to decide (*"primero miralo vos"*). SY.admin gets
  `wf_list_instances`, `wf_get_instance` and `wf_cancel_instance`.
- **Solution development is a chapter of its own** (the operator): a document on developing
  solutions, a handbook of good development practices, and for each solution a place on disk, a
  minimal manifest and how to deploy it. *"No quisiera que nazca a medias"*. Meanwhile each case
  starts from zero.
- **The gate is mine to run:** *"no me esperes a mí para esta prueba, confío en que si el bug no
  sale ahora, explota mañana"*.
