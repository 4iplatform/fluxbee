## The ED's identity

- **The operator:** stage 2's four points are agreed, but they are partial to the global design. A
  flag is not where an ED's identity lives. The data belongs in identity, with the JSON's reference
  there for every node to read: layer 2 inside Fluxbee, layer 1 on the internet. SY.deploy creates
  and keeps no ED data, as the orchestrator with the nodes. Recorded in the notes, §32.
- **Agreed:**
  - every ILK names the person it works for, and its role (`conscious`, `channel`, `agent`), in the
    SHM (version 7);
  - the person's document (the profile's JSON grown into it), kept by identity, its hash in the SHM;
  - SY.deploy executes and records deployments only.
- **The operator's addition:** the IO keeps its assignment by config, written by SY.deploy, and
  checks it against identity. Neither alone makes an IO an ED's, and a mismatch is visible. The
  conscious follows the same rule for the person it speaks as.
- **Consolidated:**
  - the design, its new §3.0, with open question 16 closed;
  - [stage 2](../../docs/digital-executive-v1-stage-2.md), rewritten whole on the new path, with
    four releases: the SHM's version 7 alone; the SDKs alone; identity, the orchestrator and
    SY.admin's identity actions; SY.deploy and its users.
