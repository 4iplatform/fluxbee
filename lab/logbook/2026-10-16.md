## The log plane: NATS dies (A-70, stage 1a in 0.1.75)

- **How it came up:** while designing stage 5 (tokens), the record was going to travel over NATS.
  The operator asked for a deep review of NATS.
  - It is an in-house broker inside each router, with weak durability.
  - It only stores what belongs to the motherbee: the turns of worker1 and of the ingress never
    reached the database.
- **The operator's decisions** (in the doc, §0):
  - NATS dies (*"quedó como algo inicial viejo"* (it was left as something old from the start));
  - the router gains a simple stream, `RT.<router>.stream@<hive>`, with the Fluxbee socket as the
    pipe;
  - best effort, with a small window (2 min and 32 MB, as constants);
  - the router never waits.
- **The design** (`docs/router-stream-v1.md`) had one review with three perspectives. It found live
  bugs:
  - the AI responses were not stored;
  - cognition processed each turn 2 to 4 times;
  - a large frame broke links;
  - SY.storage did not reconnect.
- **Stage 1a (0.1.75)** fixed them on top of today's NATS. The code review prevented a purge that
  would have deleted history: the inbox is the message log that Archi shows (Q7, the operator
  decides).
- **Q1:** the operator wants messages and cognition to be a single system, where whoever generates
  the message is its owner. It gets designed after stage 1.

## The router stream with SY.storage (A-70, stage 1b in 0.1.76)

- **Q7:** the operator decided that Archi reads `turns` (*"que lea turn es integrado mas que lo
  otro"* (reading turn is more integrated than the other)).
  - `turns` stores the whole message.
  - A one-time backfill copied what the inbox had: 542 old rows re-linked and 35 responses
    recovered.
  - The inbox deleted what had been processed more than 24 h ago.
- **The code:** each router has its stream `RT.<router>.stream@<hive>`, which delivers to the
  SY.storage of the motherbee.
  - SY.storage takes the batches with its own connection and no longer reads turns from NATS.
  - Archi, the admin (`list_streams`) and `ops.py health` (`streamlost`) show it.
- **The review** (one round, no HIGH) found two MEDIUM, fixed before the build:
  - the loss counters lied;
  - the upgrade order revived the old NATS consumer.
- **The gate on 8.x:**
  - the turns of the four hives reach the database, which before only happened with the motherbee;
  - with SY.storage absent for 71 s, everything was delivered once;
  - with more than 3 min, what expired was counted once, with its alarm;
  - a restarted router picked up what was pending;
  - the motherbee, restarted under traffic, lost nothing;
  - the health stayed clean.
- **While testing:** the SY.orchestrator watchdog brings a stopped `sy-storage` back up after 2 s.
  To simulate the absence, `systemctl mask --runtime` was used.
